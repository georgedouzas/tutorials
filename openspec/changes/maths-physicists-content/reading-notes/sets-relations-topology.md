# Reading notes: sets-relations-topology

Additions for the Zotero reading notes of `fundamentals-of-mathematics-for-physicists/sets-relations-topology`, for
the owner's review. Each line names the source location and the blocks it became.

## Section "Sets and maps" (sec-sets)

### lee2013introduction

Introduction to Smooth Manifolds, App. A, pp. 596-599 (PDF = printed + 15).

- Example A.6, p. 598: R^0 is the one-element set {0} -> exm-set-empty-singleton.
- Example A.8, p. 599: unit interval, open and closed unit balls, the n-sphere, S^0 -> exm-set-discs (with the new
  figure fig-plane-sets).
- App. A as a whole: added to the section's "For more" line.
- App. B (linear algebra) has no set or map material usable before linear algebra is introduced: not used here.

### keskivakkuri2022mathematical

Mathematical Methods for Physics, PDF = printed + 11.

- Sec. 2.3, p. 10: Map(X, Y), injection, surjection, bijection, inverse; the apples and oranges example (a
  non-injective f, a bijection g and its inverse) -> exm-inverse-table.
- Sec. 2.3, p. 10: f^{-1} is a bijection whose inverse is f -> exr-inverse-unique.
- Sec. 4.1.1, p. 99: the power set P(X) as the discrete topology -> exm-set-power-set.

### cheung2024metric

Metric Space Topology: Examples, Exercises and Solutions (PDF = printed + 18). The book has no separate chapter on

- Exercise 2.3, Part A, Problem 15, pp. 197-198: x -> e^{2 pi i x} from [0, 1) onto S^1 -> exm-bijection-angle-circle,
  exr-bijection-interval-circle.
- Exercise 2.3, Part A, Problem 17, p. 198: S c f^{-1}(f(S)) and f(f^{-1}(T)) c T -> exm-map-image-preimage-rules,

### aliev2024mathematical

Mathematical Methods for Physics: Problems and Solutions. Its eight chapters are Green's functions, boundary-value
problems for PDEs and Fourier transforms; it has no material on sets or maps. Not used for this section.

### Standard material

The remaining new blocks of sec-sets (set operations, De Morgan, power-set count, image and preimage rules, the
injectivity and surjectivity criteria, pigeonhole, counting maps and surjections, bijections of N and Z, complements,
inverse of a bijection, the physics examples on spins, hydrogen labels, particles on sites and permutations) are
standard textbook material, written without a specific source.

All other new blocks of sec-topology are standard material written without a specific source: open and closed sets

### Rule change (no forward references): items rewritten in sec-topology

exm-continuous-matrix (matrices, SO(3)) and exm-quotient-sphere-top (SO(3)/SO(2)) were removed; their references in
the chapter's closing exr-sphere-quotient-homeomorphism were reworded. exm-subspace-sphere lost SO(3);
exr-continuous-composition now uses a pendulum instead of rotations and geodesics; exr-homeomorphism-half-open proves
the needed connectedness of (0, 1) directly; exm-homeomorphism-square, exm-homeomorphism-interval, exr-quotient-moebius,
exr-finite-topologies and exr-topology-t0-count lost their links to compactness, connectedness, Hausdorff spaces,
charts and bundles; prp-finite-topologies now has its own proof instead of pointing to an exercise.
