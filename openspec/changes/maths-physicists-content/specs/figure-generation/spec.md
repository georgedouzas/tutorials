## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Every figure has a generating script
Each figure in the tutorial SHALL be produced by a committed script, and running that script SHALL regenerate the
figure file. The tutorial SHALL NOT embed hand-drawn or externally sourced images.

In Fundamentals of Mathematics for Physicists the script SHALL be a SageMath script saved next to the figure as
`figures/<name>.sage` in the chapter's folder.

#### Scenario: Figure regeneration
- **WHEN** a figure's generating script is run on a clean checkout
- **THEN** it writes the figure file at the path the document references, and the document renders with it

#### Scenario: Image without a script
- **WHEN** the document references an image file that no committed script produces
- **THEN** the content checker fails and names the image

#### Scenario: A SageMath figure script in the mathematics tutorial
- **WHEN** a chapter of Fundamentals of Mathematics for Physicists has a figure `figures/<name>.svg`
- **THEN** a SageMath script `figures/<name>.sage` in the same chapter folder writes the static image

#### Scenario: An interactive figure in the mathematics tutorial
- **WHEN** a figure script of Fundamentals of Mathematics for Physicists draws a surface, a vector field, an orbit or a potential
- **THEN** it also writes the interactive version in the chapter's `figures/interactive/`

#### Scenario: Figure scripts are not pages
- **WHEN** the site is rendered
- **THEN** the `.sage` figure scripts stay committed in the repo and none is published as a page

#### Scenario: Re-running every figure of a chapter
- **WHEN** the content checker re-runs the figure scripts of a raised chapter of the mathematics tutorial
- **THEN** each script finishes without error and writes the files the chapter references

### Requirement: Figure captions describe content and provenance
Each caption SHALL say in plain words what the figure shows, with the parameter values the reader needs to read it.
It SHALL NOT name the generating script, a file path, SageMath or any other tool, and SHALL be at most two sentences.
This applies to every tutorial.

#### Scenario: Caption inspected
- **WHEN** a reader reads a figure caption
- **THEN** it says what is depicted and with which parameters, in at most two sentences, and names no script, file or tool

#### Scenario: A caption with provenance
- **WHEN** the content checker finds a caption containing "Generated by", a `.sage` or `.py` file name, or the words Sage or SageMath
- **THEN** it reports the caption with its page and figure label

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Figures are audited when a chapter is raised
When a chapter of Fundamentals of Mathematics for Physicists is raised, each of its figures SHALL be checked:

- does it show what the text and the caption say;
- does it use the site notation;
- is it legible in light and dark mode at the width it is shown;
- does it carry a point the text makes?

A figure that fails SHALL be redrawn or removed. Each statement whose object can be drawn and that has no figure SHALL
get one among its examples.

#### Scenario: A figure in the old notation
- **WHEN** a figure's labels use a symbol that differs from the notation page
- **THEN** its script is changed to the site symbol and the figure is regenerated

#### Scenario: A drawable notion without a figure
- **WHEN** a raised chapter defines a notion that can be drawn (a set in the plane, a curve, a surface, a vector field, a spectrum, a group orbit) and none of its examples has a figure
- **THEN** a figure is added to one of its examples, with a script and a simple caption

#### Scenario: Dark mode
- **WHEN** a reader views a figure of a raised chapter in dark mode
- **THEN** its lines, labels and colours stay visible against the dark background
