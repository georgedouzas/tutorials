# Proposal

## Why

Fundamentals of Mathematics for Physicists is thinner than its chapter template asks:

- **Examples and exercises.** The template asks for ten examples and ten exercises after each statement, but every
  statement has about three of each. The textbooks in the Zotero library offer far more worked material than the
  chapters use.
- **Solutions.** About half of the 1,746 solutions show a SageMath computation and half do not, and the two forms are
  not visually separated.
- **Figures.**
  - Their Sage scripts were removed from the repo (commit `99a3d9a`), so none of the 115 figures can be regenerated.
  - About 100 captions still end with "Generated by `figures/<name>.sage`", which points to a file that no longer
    exists.
  - Many captions are long.
- **Missing topics.** The coverage record of `tutorials-missing-chapters` lists 20 topics missing from existing
  chapters (for example determinants, eigenvalues, vector calculus, characters). It also lists two approved chapters
  that are still placeholders: Calculus of variations, and Homotopy and cohomology.

Counts at the time of this change, over the 15 written chapters:

| Item | Count |
|---|---|
| Statements | 544 |
| Examples | 1,667 |
| Exercises | 1,746 |
| Examples missing for ten per statement | 3,776 |
| Exercises missing for ten per statement | 3,702 |

## What Changes

- **Ten and ten.** In this tutorial, every `def-`, `thm-`, `lem-`, `prp-` and `cor-` block is followed by exactly
  ten examples of increasing complexity and exactly ten exercises of increasing difficulty. The new material comes
  from the Zotero textbooks mapped to the chapter and is cited at the point of use.
- **Two solutions per exercise.** Every exercise solution has two tabs:
  - **Proof:** the theoretical solution.
  - **SageMath:** code with the output it actually produced. The tab has its own colour and label.

  When an exercise asks for a proof, the SageMath tab checks the claim on a concrete case and says that it is a
  check, not a proof.
- **Figures.**
  - Every figure of the tutorial is audited: is it correct, is it in the site notation, is it legible in light and
    dark mode, and does it carry its point?
  - Every figure is regenerated by a SageMath script saved next to it as `figures/<name>.sage`.
  - Missing figures are added where a notion can be drawn.
- **Simple captions.**
  - **Rule:** captions say what the figure shows in plain words, with no script names, file paths or tool names.
    The rule applies site-wide.
  - **In this change:** the captions of the other physics tutorials are shortened as well. Their figures are not
    regenerated.
- **Expanded content.**
  - The 20 recorded topics are added to the existing chapters as new sections.
  - The two approved planned chapters are written.
  - Further content is added where a chapter's mapped textbooks show a gap.
  - Everything follows the chapter template and the ten-and-ten rule.
- **A content checker.** A new script `_filters/check_content.py` does three things:
  - counts examples and exercises per statement;
  - checks that each solution has both tabs and that each caption follows the rule;
  - re-runs every SageMath tab and every figure script and compares their outputs with what the page shows.
- **Pilot first.**
  - Linear algebra is done first and reviewed by the owner.
  - The other chapters follow one at a time in reading order.
  - The checker applies the new rules only to chapters listed in `fundamentals-of-mathematics-for-physicists/_raised.txt`,
    so the build stays green at every step.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `tutorial-content`:
  - ten examples and ten exercises per statement in Fundamentals of Mathematics for Physicists;
  - Proof and SageMath tabs in every exercise solution there;
  - the recorded section gaps and planned chapters written.
- `figure-generation`:
  - a figure's script lives next to the figure as `figures/<name>.sage`;
  - captions are simple and name no script or tool;
  - the content checker re-runs the scripts.
- `physics-tutorials`: the SageMath examples requirement now covers every exercise solution in the mathematics
  tutorial, not only selected examples.
- `chapter-coverage`: a planned chapter stops being a placeholder once it is written, and the record's section gaps are
  marked done.

## Impact

- **Changed files:**
  - the 15 written chapters, and the two placeholder chapters, of `fundamentals-of-mathematics-for-physicists/`;
  - their `figures/`, which gain `.sage` scripts;
  - `_quarto.yml`: the two "planned" badges are removed;
  - `theme.scss`: the solution tabs;
  - new: `_filters/check_content.py` and `fundamentals-of-mathematics-for-physicists/_raised.txt`;
  - the glossary and notation pages, wherever new terms or symbols are added.
- **Other tutorials:** only their captions change (about 270 captions in Theoretical Physics and Kaluza-Klein).
- **Zotero:** the reading notes of the mapped textbooks gain the sections and problems used. Every Zotero write is
  shown to the owner first.
- **Size and cost:**
  - about 7,500 new blocks for the existing chapters, plus the new sections and chapters (an estimated 1,500 more);
  - each chapter roughly triples in length, but the Examples and Exercises folds keep the line of argument short;
  - at the rate of the last textbook pass, about 6k agent tokens per block, this is in the order of 55M agent tokens;
  - one task per chapter, so the owner can stop after any chapter.
- **Dependencies:** SageMath 10.9 is installed locally (`/usr/local/bin/sage`). The site still renders without Sage,
  because outputs are recorded in the pages.
