## Conventions used in this tutorial

Checked against `sec-notation` in `fundamentals-of-mathematics-for-machine-learning/mathematical-and-statistical-foundations/index.qmd`,
which says it applies to "the remainder of these tutorials". Abbreviations: F = foundations-of-learning-theory,
S = supervised-learning, U = unsupervised-learning, R = reinforcement-learning; numbers are line numbers in `<chapter>/index.qmd`.

- **Physics conventions** (metric signature, Fourier sign and 2π, natural units, inner-product linearity, curvature signs,
  Lie-algebra normalisation): not used. No hits in any chapter.
- **Bold vectors and matrices:** the tutorial's own convention, used about 1,850 times; it matches `sec-notation`. Exceptions
  that write vectors or matrices plain in their own formulas:
  - F online convex optimisation, OGD, FTRL and online-to-batch (`w_t`, `\bar w`; F3315-3420, 3540, 3750);
  - F LinUCB (`\hat\theta_t`, `x_{t,a}`, `A_t`, `I`; F3703-3736);
  - F PCA exercises (plain `\Sigma`; F2938-2945);
  - R Bellman matrix form (`P_\pi`, `r_\pi`, `v_k`, `I`, `\Pi`, `\tilde r`; R869-914 and R1018-1052), next to the bold
    `\mathbf{v}_\pi`, `\mathbf{P}_\pi` of R844-929.

  The PAC sections of F write the domain point `x` plain, which is fine because `\mathcal X` is an arbitrary set.
- **Expectation:** `\mathbb{E}` everywhere (151 uses), often with a subscripted distribution (`\mathbb{E}_\pi`,
  `\mathbb{E}_{S\sim\mathcal D^m}`). This matches the site default. The one exception, plain `E[...]`, is inside a code block of
  pseudocode (S4201).
- **Probability:** `\mathbb{P}[\cdot]` in F (17 uses, Shalev-Shwartz style), but `P(\cdot)` in the same chapter (F1265, F2277) and
  in S, R and `sec-notation`. For densities and PMFs, S writes `P(\mathbf{x}\mid y=k)` (about 80 uses), while U and R write
  `p(\cdot)`, as `sec-notation` does.
- **Transpose:** `^T` (S, U, R, 250 uses), as in `sec-notation`. F uses `^\top` (14 uses: F208, F2909-2998, F3703-3736).
- **Inner product:** `\langle\mathbf{w},\mathbf{x}\rangle` in F (perceptron, OGD), `\mathbf{w}^T\mathbf{x}` elsewhere. `sec-notation`
  allows both. No linearity convention arises, because everything is real.
- **Indicator:** `\mathbb{1}[\cdot]` (F288, F545), `\mathbb{1}_{x\ge a}` (F432) and `\mathbb{1}_{[\cdot]}` (S, R3737).
  `sec-notation` writes `\mathbb{1}_{\cdot}`.
- **Logarithm:** F writes the natural logarithm as `\ln` (37 uses, AIC/BIC, Hedge) and `\log_2` for code lengths. The other
  chapters use `\log`, the `sec-notation` default, with occasional `\ln` (U271, U2118, R1338).
- **Sample size:** `n` in S and U and in F's statistical-learning part (F283-330, F1555, F1703). `m` in F's PAC, VC, Rademacher,
  stability and nonuniform-learning parts (F413 onward, Shalev-Shwartz). `N` for datasets in R (R3501) and in the S CLIP batch.
  `sec-notation` says `n`.
- **Number of features:** `p` in S linear and generative models (S57, S736-1000) and in F208. `d` in S neural networks
  (S2397, S3341), in U and in F feature selection. `sec-notation` allows "p or d".
- **Loss:** `\ell` with argument orders `\ell(h,z)` (F1362, `sec-notation`), `\ell(\hat y_t,y_t)` (F3215) and `\ell(y_i,f(\mathbf x_i))`
  (S2255, S2265). Risk is written `L_{\mathcal D}(h)` and `L_S(h)` in F, as in `sec-notation`. In S, U and R, loss and objective
  functions are `L(\cdot)`, `\mathcal L_{\cdot}` or `J(\cdot)`.
- **Parameters:** `\boldsymbol\theta` for network and policy parameters, `\mathbf w` for linear-model and value-function weights.
  `sec-notation` allows both. Plain `\theta` appears as a parameter in F (NML, autoencoder F2954), in S RLHF/DPO (`\pi_\theta`,
  S4190-4322) and in R offline RL (`Q_\theta`, R3597-3761).
- **Reinforcement learning:** Sutton-Barto conventions. Random variables are `S_t, A_t, R_{t+1}`, with the reward indexed `t+1`.
  True values are `v_\pi, q_\pi, v_*, q_*` and estimates are `V, Q, \hat v, \hat q`. The later sections (GAE, PPO, SAC,
  offline: R3168 onward) switch to lowercase `s_t, a_t, r_t`, with the reward indexed `t`, as in the deep-RL papers.
- **Random variables uppercase:** followed in F and in the early R sections. S uses `P(y=k\mid\mathbf{x})` with lowercase
  values, as is usual.
- **Operator typography:** `\text{Var}`, `\text{Cov}`, `\text{diag}` (`sec-notation`) next to `\mathrm{Cov}` (U1689, U2259-2289)
  and `\mathrm{diag}` (U). The trace is written `\mathrm{tr}` (U1658-1687), `\operatorname{tr}` (S218-223) and `\text{tr}` in
  `sec-notation`. Rank is `\text{rank}` (S4542), against `\text{rk}` in `sec-notation`. KL divergence appears as `D_{KL}` (F3004),
  `D_{\text{KL}}` (R3303) and `\text{KL}` (S4264, U2046).
- **Comparison-only passages:** none found. Every variant above is used in the chapters' own formulas.
